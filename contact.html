Why the form is simple

The form starts the connection. The conversation creates the clarity.

I would rather understand what you are trying to accomplish in a real conversation than ask you to complete a long intake form before we have even met.

For speaking inquiries, choose the type of opportunity and give me the basics. Audience, goals, format, timing, travel, and the rest can be worked through on a call.
