The idea behind the work

Stop screening. Start understanding.

A résumé can show where someone has been. A better conversation helps uncover what those experiences meant, what the person learned, what they need next, and whether the opportunity truly fits.

  • Understand motivation before pitching the job
  • Build alignment before asking for commitment
  • Give hiring managers better information to decide
  • Treat conversation quality as part of hiring quality
