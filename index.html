Better hiring is not about making the process softer. It is about making the information better. Better questions create better understanding. Better understanding creates better alignment. Better alignment creates better decisions.

The work behind the ideas

I’m still in the arena.

I’ve worked in human resources and recruiting since 2009, across consulting, high-volume hiring, operational recruiting, leadership, and talent acquisition management.

I’m not trying to build a polished philosophy that only works on a stage. The writing and frameworks here come from candidate calls, hiring-manager conversations, offers, missed hires, retention questions, leadership decisions, and the moments where the process either becomes human or loses the person entirely.

The point is not to make hiring complicated. It is to stop pretending that a shallow process gives us deep information.
